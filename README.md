# FSU CELLS Research Directory

**Designed and built by Macin Bennetts**, Scholarship & Internship Chair, FSU CELLS (Connecting Experimental, Lab, and Life Sciences).

A guide for CELLS members to undergraduate research at Florida State. It covers labs, faculty leads, programs and UROP projects, with a saved shortlist that tracks outreach and follow-ups, a research calendar with add-to-calendar files, and outreach templates.

- Live site: https://myweb.fsu.edu/mnb23f/
