Undergraduate Research Opportunities
Dewan Lab 
The Dewan Lab is always looking for smart, reliable, hard-working undergraduates who are looking to gain research experience. A minimum of two trimester commitment (two years) and enrollment for DIS credit is required.
Anyone is welcome to apply if they meet the previous two criteria. We are specifically looking for individuals to fill the following roles; however, you are still welcome to and encouraged to apply if not interested in the following two specific roles:
A student interested in assisting with human participant interviews, interview transcription, data entry and cleaning, usability testing, and intervention studies.
A student who is free on Saturdays and Sundays to help care for our experimental mouse colony.
If interested, please fill out the Qualtrics survey at the following link: Dewan Lab Undergraduate Research Assistant Interest Form . If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the lab manager (pauley@psy.fsu.edu) or Dr. Dewan (dewan@psy.fsu.edu) directly.
