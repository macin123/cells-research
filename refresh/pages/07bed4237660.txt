Research Specialties
Bioanalytical, Chemical Biology, Structural Biology, Synthesis and Catalysis
Research Interest
Research in Wen Zhu's laboratory focuses on translating the fundamental understanding of enzyme catalysis into the knowledge needed for innovative drug discovery. We use genome mining, mechanistic enzymology, structural biology, and biophysical tools to investigate how metallocofactors and protein motions control catalysis.
