Project Description
I am seeking the assistance of a UROP student to support an ongoing and time-sensitive literature review project. Under the guidance of the Principal Investigator, the student will be responsible for systematically screening 3,040 articles according to established inclusion and exclusion criteria and documenting eligibility determinations. Additional research-related responsibilities may be assigned as needed to facilitate the progression, synthesis, and successful completion of the literature review.
Research Tasks: Literature Review 
Skills that research assistant(s) may need: We will provide the selected UROP student with the training, guidance, and resources necessary to successfully perform assigned responsibilities.
Required Skills
Strong attention to detail and ability to follow established protocols.
Ability to apply inclusion and exclusion criteria consistently when screening research articles.
Basic understanding of research methods and scholarly literature.
Strong organizational and time-management skills.
Effective written and verbal communication skills.
Ability to ask questions and seek clarification when needed.
Ability to work independently while collaborating with the Principal Investigator.
Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and academic database searches.
Recommended Skills
Prior experience assisting with literature reviews, research projects, or evidence synthesis.
Familiarity with databases such as PubMed, CINAHL, PsycINFO, Scopus, or Web of Science.
Knowledge of citation management software (e.g., EndNote, Zotero, or Mendeley).
Experience with systematic, scoping, or integrative reviews.
Ability to critically evaluate and summarize scientific literature.
Interest in nursing, public health, mental health, HIV research, or related health science fields.
Demonstrated initiative, curiosity, and willingness to learn new research methodologies.
Mentoring Philosophy
The UROP student will receive individualized mentorship from the Principal Investigator throughout the literature review process. Training will be provided on article screening, application of inclusion and exclusion criteria, research best practices, and literature synthesis. Regular meetings will offer opportunities for feedback, skill development, and discussion of questions or challenges encountered during the project. The student will be encouraged to develop critical thinking, scientific inquiry, and evidence appraisal skills. This mentorship experience is designed to foster research competence, professional growth, and preparation for future academic and research opportunities.
