Undergraduate Research Opportunities
Varela Lab 
Directed Independent Study (DIS) students can expect to work closely with graduate students and/or post-docs to assist with electrode preparation, rodent surgeries, behavioral data collection, and data analysis. Undergraduate students are expected to participate in weekly lab meetings whenever possible and to present their work for discussion with the lab at least once per semester. Requirements:
Minimum GPA of 3.0
Sophomore, Junior, or Senior
Minimum dedication of 10 h/week
Interest in pursuing graduate level research training
Ability to learn fast and work as part of a team
