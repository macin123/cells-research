Project Description
Architectured materials are a generic term that describes a design that incorporates a combination of different material systems or spaces which offer greater functionality than either of the constituents. Most current materials are nature-inspired, such as seashells and muscle structures. 
In this project, you will investigate the impact of magnetic fields on a class of materials that react to a magnetic field and develop architectured material structures. Our goal is to develop strong, lightweight materials for deep space applications where temperatures range from -410F to 210F.

Mentoring Philosophy
I believe you are highly capable and have a bright future, and I want to support you. 
My goal is to support and nurture your interests and enthusiasm across engineering and science by providing interactive environments for learning and creating opportunities: 
a) to conduct independent but guided research in my group- on specific topics in the extreme environment space
b) to learn to present to a broad spectrum of audiences- by regular presentations within the group and in other settings 
c) to broaden future horizons- by providing information and encouragement to explore national lab, company, and grad school opportunities. 
d) to provide a safe space to learn and develop
e) to encourage ownership and promote accountability. 
Most importantly, I will encourage you and want you to succeed. 
Shreyas Balachandran
