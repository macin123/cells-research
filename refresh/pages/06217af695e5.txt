Biosketch
Dr. Boris Kantor is the Director of the IPRD Viral Vector Core Facility and a Professor of Biomedical Sciences at the FSU College of Medicine. Prior to joining the IPRD in July 2025, Dr. Kantor directed the Viral Vector Core at Duke University. Trained in medicine and neuroscience, he earned his Ph.D. from The Hebrew University of Jerusalem and completed postdoctoral training at the University of North Carolina Gene Therapy Center. Dr. Kantor is internationally recognized for his expertise in the design, development, and production of viral vectors, including adeno-associated and lentiviral systems, for preclinical and translational research. His work spans the creation of innovative gene therapy platforms for neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimer's and Parkinson's, epigenome editing strategies for precision medicine, and large-scale vector manufacturing for academic and industry partners. Dr. Kantor brings extensive technical knowledge and a track record of innovation to the IPRD Viral Vector Core, advancing its mission to provide cutting-edge vector solutions for diverse biomedical applications.
Education

&bull; Department of Human Molecular Genetics at Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Israel-Ph.D. 2004
&bull; Gene Therapy Center at University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill- Postdoctoral Fellowship 2009
