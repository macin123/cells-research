0 6 .1 1 .2026 The Irianto Lab received the Notice of Award for our R01 grant ( 1R01CA297178 ) from the National Cancer Institute (NCI), NIH!! This is a major milestone for the lab and would not have been possible without the contributions and support of our current and former lab members, as well as colleagues and collaborators across the department, university, and beyond. 
This multidisciplinary project brings together Tristan Driscoll (FAMU-FSU College of Engineering), Katarzyna Rejniak (Moffitt Cancer Center), Chang-il Hwang (UC Davis), and Jose I Diaz (FSU College of Medicine). Together, we will integrate bioengineering, cell biology, mathematical modeling, cancer biology, and pathology to investigate pancreatic cancer progression using human-derived pancreatic organoid models.
