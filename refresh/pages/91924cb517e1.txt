Full-Time Lab Technician
A full-time (40 hours/week) lab technician position is available immediately in Dr. Roberto Vincis' laboratory in the Department of Biological Science and Program in Neuroscience at Florida State University. Our research focuses on the neural mechanisms underlying taste, flavor, and feeding behavior. We use a combination of genetic, optogenetic, chemogenetic, electrophysiological, optical imaging, and behavioral techniques in mice to explore how the brain processes the sensory and rewarding features of food and uses them to guide eating decisions.
Responsibilities 
Assisting with, and then independently running, behavioral experiments in mice.
General lab upkeep, logistics, supplies ordering, and supporting ongoing research.
The candidate will also be given the opportunity to lead their own project, potentially leading to a scientific publication.
Qualifications 
Required: 
A four-year B.S. or B.A. degree (or higher) in the life or physical sciences.
Previous experience working with laboratory animals, ideally rodents.
Strong time-management skills, a professional attitude, a desire to learn new techniques, and the ability to work with a diverse team of students and researchers.
A strong interest in laboratory research.
Preferred: 
Previous experience with behavioral or histological techniques.
Previous coursework in genetics, biology, chemistry, research techniques, or animal handling/procedures.
How to Apply 
Compensation is based on experience, in accordance with university policy. To apply, please send your CV and one letter of recommendation to rvincis@fsu.edu with the subject line "Lab Technician Application - [Your Name]". There is no fixed deadline; applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis, and the position will remain open until it is filled.
