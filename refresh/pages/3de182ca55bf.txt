Project Description
The goal of the project is to understand the superconducting materials including high temperature superconductors of Bi-2212 and REBCO, and low temperature superconductor of Nb3Sn, to address challenges encountered during the magnet development efforts. We will carry out comprehensive characterizations to understand the materials and provide conductor information to the magnet designer and the conductor manufacturers to further improve conductor performance. 
Research Tasks: The task covers literature review, data collection and data analysis. The students will work in the lab and in the office.
Skills that research assistant(s) may need: Recommended: Microsoft word, Inventor, and lab working experience
Mentoring Philosophy
The students will work based on individual interests. The students will learn multiple skills in an open environment. They will have opportunities developing their interests and chose the specific project. Students are encouraged to go to the lab making mistakes and learning from that,
