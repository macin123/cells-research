Attraction and Close Relationships (ACR) Lab 
Andrea Meltzer, Ph.D. will be accepting applications to be a DIS in her lab for the Spring 2027 semester. Research in the Meltzer Lab examines the psychological processes that underlie the human desire to obtain and maintain romantic relationships. You can learn more about the research by visiting the lab website at andreameltzer.com. 
Benefits of joining the Meltzer Lab include: 
Direct, hands-on experience running psychological studies, working with data, discussing research study design
Exposure to social psychological literature on attraction, dating and long-term romantic relationships
Professional development and guidance on getting into graduate school
Requirements: 
Minimum overall GPA of 3.2
Ability to commit to a minimum of two semesters
Ability to attend meetings throughout the semester (to be scheduled before the upcoming semester; currently held on Fridays at 10:00 AM)
Although not required, preference will be given to students who have taken and earned excellent grades in (a) Research Methods, and (b) Social Psychology
If you are interested, please use this link to submit your application.
