SMARTer Weight-loss Study 
The research team, under the guidance of Dr. Bonnie Spring, develops and tests interventions to promote healthy diet and physical activity changes using connected smartphone applications, wearable devices, and health coaching. We aim to reduce risk factors for chronic disease by optimizing innovative technology-supported interventions to catalyze healthy change. 
DIS students will have the opportunity to work with other research staff to screen study candidates for eligibility, participate in community engagement events, perform assessments, conduct literature reviews, prepare documents IRB submissions, etc. 
Students interested in this opportunity should fill out the following Google form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSflvLRNsO4Fok5rSucUQgVenggDHiteZzjbbsFnmTsapAfSUA/viewform 
For more information, contact the Research Project Manager, Dr. Keri Gladhill at keri.gladhill@med.fsu.edu.
