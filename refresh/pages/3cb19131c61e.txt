Undergraduate Research Opportunities
Nee Lab 
The Nee Lab for Cognitive Neuroscience is recruiting reliable and motivated undergraduates to assist researchers with their studies. The Nee Lab studies working memory and cognitive control using techniques like functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI), electroencephalography (EEG) and transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS). DIS students get hands-on experience conducting human research studies as well as exposure to data analysis, coding and behavioral methods. To be considered, prospective members must have a minimum GPA of 3.3 and be able to work independently and coordinate with a group. All applicants meeting these requirements are encouraged to apply. Applicants studying a relevant field (e.g. Psychology, Neuroscience, Computer Science, Statistics) are preferred. Interested students should email their CV/Resume, unofficial transcript, and a letter of intent to neesubjects@psy.fsu.edu 
**Face-to-face and remote DIS options available**
