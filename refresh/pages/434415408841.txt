Welcome to the Wang Lab! 
Our lab is conducting exciting research on the molecular programs of diabetes.  
We are applying highly innovative single-cell multiomics as well as functional genomics approaches to elucidate pathways involved in islet dysfunction and diabetes pathogenesis.  
We are also developing bioinformatics tools to deep analyze single-cell RNA-seq data to derive further biological insights.  
We aim to discover novel targets that lead to the effective treatment of diabetes.
