Kalkreuter Lab (March 2026) 
Join Us! 
We are actively recruiting exceptional graduate students to join the lab! 
​ 
Areas include: 
Synthetic Biology 
Chemical Biology 
Natural Products 
Protein Engineering 
Enzymology 
Microbiology 
Bioinformatics 
Structural Biology 
​ 
Postdoctoral candidates: 
Send a cover letter, a CV, and the names of at least two recommenders. 
​ ​ 
Graduate students: 
Reach out directly to schedule a meeting to discuss if and only if you have already been accepted into the program. 
​ 
If you have not joined the department, please send an application to the department. I cannot directly accept students. 
​ 
Undergraduate students: 
Send a cover letter and a transcript.
