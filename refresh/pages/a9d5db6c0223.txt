For Students
Are you an undergraduate, graduate, or doctoral student at FSU who is interested in earning more research experience?
Meet the current lab students! 
Undergraduate opportunities: 
There are several options available for undergraduate students to get involved in the CLaSS Lab.  Volunteer opportunities require at least 3 hours of availability per week.  Students must also complete CITI training  before working in the lab.  Please complete this training and send the certificate of completion to Dr. Farquharson kfarquharson@fsu.edu.
Students who are interested in an undergraduate thesis option can consider the  Honors in the Major  program or the  Honors Program. 
Graduate opportunities: 
Graduate students in the School of Communication Science and Disorders must complete some form of research experience.  The CLaSS Lab has several spots available to support these research experiences, including Master's theses.
Doctoral opportunities: 
Please email kelly.farquharson@cci.fsu.edu to discuss opportunities for doctoral students or post-doctoral research fellows.
