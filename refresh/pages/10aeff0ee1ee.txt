Welcome to the Mohammadigoushki Research Group in the Department of Chemical & Biomedical Engineering at the FAMU-FSU College of Engineering. 
Founded in August 2016, our research group is dedicated to scientific studies at the interface of chemical engineering, mechanical engineering, material science, and physics. 
Openings: We are looking for talented students at the undergraduate and graduate (MS and PhD) levels. Please contact Dr. Hadi directly at hadi.moham@eng.famu.fsu.edu .
