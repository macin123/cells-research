Project Description
This undergraduate research opportunity is part of a project focused on advancing data literacy education for high school and community college students in the Tallahassee area. The project seeks to develop, deliver, and evaluate educational programs that help learners build skills in understanding, interpreting, analyzing, and communicating with data while preparing them for data-intensive academic pathways, careers, and everyday decision-making.
Working closely with the project team, the UROP student will contribute to the evaluation of pilot data literacy workshops by assisting with the development, collection, and analysis of instructional assessment data, including pre- and post-training surveys used to measure learning outcomes. Responsibilities will also include conducting background research on data literacy education, instructional assessment, and evidence-based teaching practices for high school and community college audiences. Additional opportunities include assisting with the development of facilitator guides and instructional materials that support educators in delivering data literacy training.
Participation in this project will provide hands-on experience in educational research, program evaluation, quantitative analysis, literature searching, and curriculum development while contributing to a community-focused initiative aimed at strengthening data literacy skills across learner populations.

Mentoring Philosophy
I believe effective mentorship is built on collaboration, mutual respect, and shared learning. Together, we will establish clear goals and expectations that support a positive and encouraging environment where students can develop research skills, learn from challenges, and grow in confidence as scholars and professionals. I view mentorship as a partnership that promotes both personal and professional growth while helping students achieve their academic and career goals.
