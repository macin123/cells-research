POSITIONS WITHIN THE DEWAN LAB
Graduate Students 
The Dewan Lab is seeking graduate students interested in the molecular and cellular basis of sensory perception. Our lab uses molecular, in vivo imaging, and behavioral techniques to understand olfactory perception. Interested applicants should apply through the Program in Neuroscience . 
Undergraduate Students 
We are always looking for smart, reliable , hard-working undergraduates who are looking to gain research experience. A minimum of two trimester commitment (two years) and enrollment for DIS credit is required . If interested, please fill out the Qualtrics survey at the following link: Dewan Lab Undergraduate Research Assistant Interest Form . If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the lab manager (pauley@psy.fsu.edu) or Dr. Dewan (dewan@psy.fsu.edu) directly. 
Federal Work Study Students 
The Dewan Lab currently has openings for Federal Work Study Students to start during the 2025-2026 school year.
Search for job ID 57697 using the instructions found here (FSU Financial Aid) to apply!
