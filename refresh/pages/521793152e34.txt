Interested in O ur R esearch? 
Reach out to Professor Ramamoorthy by email, please share your CV, previous research experience, and your motivation for applying. We also invite you to engage with our lab members, who are always ready to discuss lab activities and life in Tallahassee (Florida). We believe in fostering a supportive and collaborative environment for all our researchers. 
Undergraduate students 
We are looking for outstanding undergraduate researchers from various disciplines, including Biomedical and Chemical Engineering, Physics, Materials, Biophysics, Chemistry, Biology, and Neuroscience, to participate in exciting research projects in our lab.  
Professor Ramamoorthy is particularly interested in undergraduate research. Our graduate students and postdoctoral fellows are excellent mentors. Most of our undergraduate researchers have been successful, published excellent papers, and have gone on to pursue medical school, graduate school, and industrial jobs.  
Graduate students 
We are seeking highly motivated and dynamic graduate students who will play a crucial role in our various interdisciplinary research projects. Your contributions will be instrumental in advancing our research and making a significant impact in your field of interest. 
Postdocs 
If you are interested in our research, please get in touch with Professor Ramamoorthy.
