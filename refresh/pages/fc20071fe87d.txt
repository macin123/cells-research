Job Description
Deirdre Mc Carthy is Research Faculty in the Department of Biomedical Sciences , Assistant Director of The Florida Institute for Pediatric Rare Diseases and member of The Center for Brain Repair . Her research interests focus on developmental and behavioral neuroscience.
Biosketch
Deirdre received her BSc. in Biochemistry from University College Cork, Ireland. She trained at Massachusettes General Hospital and Harvard Medical School with Dr. Pradeep Bhide and joined the faculty at FSU, College of Medicine in 2011
Service
CoM Wellness Committee June 2024- present
FSU, CoM Equipment Committee (2022-2023)
