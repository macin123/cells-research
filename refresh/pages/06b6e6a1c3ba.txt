Prospective DIS 
Interested in doing a DIS (Directed Individual Study) in social psychology? 
The Plant Lab provides hands-on opportunities for motivated and conscientious FSU undergraduates to assist with various aspects of the research process. We also provide important support for our DIS students interested in or preparing for graduate school. 
​ 
Take a look at our Areas of Research page to determine if our research is of interest to you. If you are interested in applying to join the lab, please follow the instructions below and complete an application. Email our lab manager, Bayla Thompson ( bkthompson@psy.fsu.edu ) if you have any questions. 
Lab Application 
Required qualifications for prospective DIS: 
Sophomore, Junior, or Senior student at time of participation 
Taken or enrolled in Social Psychology at time of participation 
Able to attend Friday lab meetings (2:30 - 3:30pm EST) 
Able to commit 9-10 hours per week to the course (on average) 
Psychology major (minors also considered) 
​ 
Preferred (but not required) qualifications for prospective DIS: 
Overall GPA of 3.0+ (and/or Psychology GPA of 3.25+) 
Taken or enrolled in Research Methods at time of participation 
​ 
Our application window for Spring 2027 participation is closed. 
Semester Enrollment & Application Windows: For the fall semester enrollment, our application window is approximately March 1-15th, and for the spring semester enrollment, our application window is approximately October 1-15th. To Volunteer: We usually allow students to volunteer in the lab once they've completed at least 2 semesters of DIS credit.
