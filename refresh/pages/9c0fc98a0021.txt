Join Us
We are always looking for talented Ph.D., master, and undergraduate students interested in research on computer security, computer systems, and AI safety.
For Prospective PhD Students
TML 2 has openings for Ph.D. students to work on computer architecture, computer security and AI safety research.
How to be considered
Submit a complete official application to the FSU Computer Science graduate program link . For more information, refer to this .
Include all required materials:
transcripts
CV
statement of purpose
required test scores (if applicable)
all required recommendation letters
payment of the application fee (or an officially approved fee waiver)
List me as a potential advisor and clearly describe your research interests and relevant background.
Important
Like other research universities, FSU can only make admission and funding decisions based on complete applications in the official system.
I do not evaluate profiles, make offers, or schedule interviews based solely on email, CVs, or incomplete files.
Applicants are fully responsible for ensuring that their documents and letters are submitted on time.
Please note that FSU’s up-front cost may be higher for international students than many peers because the credential evaluation is required before review.
Fee waivers
Requests for application fee waivers are handled only by the Graduate School according to their official policies. Individual faculty (including me) cannot override or grant waivers.
Contact
After you have submitted a complete application, you may email me with:
your full name,
your application ID,
a short paragraph decribing your background and research interests.
For Current FSU Undergrad, Master, and PhD Students
If you are looking for research opportunities or collaboration, contact mluo@fsu.edu.
