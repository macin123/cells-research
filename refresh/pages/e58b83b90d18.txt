Undergraduate Research Opportunities
Motivated Social Cognition Lab 
Dr. Irmak Olcaysoy Okten is accepting DIS research assistants for the Motivated Social Cognition (MSC) Lab in the Department of Psychology, starting from Fall 2022. The research in the MSC lab will focus on social cognitive and motivational processes underlying preconceived notions and behaviors. More details about the research are available at https://www.motivatedsocialcognition.com/ .
Selected students will get hands-on experience and have the opportunity to contribute to research through literature review, study design, data collection, data cleaning and analysis, and content coding. Interested students can apply by using this link .
