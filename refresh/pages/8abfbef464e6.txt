Get Involved
Research participants: 
If you are an individual with aphasia or a caregiver/friend/family member of an individual with aphasia and would like to learn  about opportunities to participate in aphasia research studies, please call  850-644-4088 or email fsuaphasialab@gmail.com 
Speech-Language Pathologists: 
If you are an SLP or related professional working with individuals with aphasia and would like to refer patients and/or learn about opportunities to collaborate with the FSU Aphasia Research Lab, please contact us at 850-644-4088 or fsuaphasialab@gmail.com 
Students: 
If you are an undergraduate or graduate student interested in volunteering in the FSU Aphasia Research Lab, please email Dr. Elizabeth Madden at ebmadden@fsu.edu
