Direct Individual Study (DIS)
Undergraduates are welcome to take Direct Individual Study (DIS) courses through Behavioral Sciences and Social Medicine faculty research laboratories. Credit hours are variable. If a Psychology major needs the credits toward a major, they can sign up under a PSY course number. Contact the instructor for additional details. Permission of the instructor is required to sign up.
