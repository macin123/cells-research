Project Description
AI is a reality and is omnipresent in higher education, bringing unprecedented changes to the sector in the 21st century (Educause, 2024). This project will explore the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI), mental health, and career development among college students. We will examine the impact of AI resources on these students’ well-being and career readiness during their transition to postsecondary education in the era of AI.
Research Tasks: • 1. Literature review
• 2. IRB application
• 3. AI app development
• 4. Data collection and analysis
• 5. Manuscript writing
Skills that research assistant(s) may need: • 1. Good writing skills (required)
• 2. Literature review experiences (required)
• 3. APA format style (recommended)
• 4. Data collection and analysis (recommended)
• 5. Knowledge of and experience with Qualtrics and SPSS (recommended)
• 6. Skills in AI app development and coding (recommended)
Mentoring Philosophy
I feel honored to receive the UROP mentor award in 2025. Providing timely advising and generous mentoring for students on their research is an important aspect of my commitment to student development. I take a developmental mentoring approach. During the initial process, I help students familiarize themselves with relevant literature and engage them in IRB training. These activities help them connect with the literature and the research team. Over time, I have been assigned more responsibilities in data collection, analysis, and writing. I constantly involve students in conference presentations and publications
