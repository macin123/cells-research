Research Interests
Educational psychology, learning disabilities, quantitative methods and research design, reading and language comprehension.
Current Research
Individual differences in reading skill development. Specifically, Chris Schatschneider is interested in the identification of skills that are related to the acquisition of reading ability and the use of these skills to identify children who are at-risk for early reading problems. He also has a strong interest in research design, measurement, and statistical methodology with a particular focus on multi-level modeling and item response theory.
Lab Description
Development of reading skills; prediction and identification of children with reading disabilities; research design, measurement, and quantitative methods such as hierarchical linear modeling item response theory models.
