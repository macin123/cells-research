Undergraduate Research Opportunities
Patrick CNS Lab 
The Patrick Lab is currently accepting applications for new undergraduate DIS students! DIS students will gain hands-on experience with collecting electroencephalography (EEG) data and conducting human research study visits. Additional opportunities for learning how to perform PCL-SV (Psychopathy Checklist – Screening Version) ratings may also be available.
The Patrick Lab broadly focuses on psychopathy and externalizing disorders and uses a variety of methods to research these topics. Undergraduate DIS are encouraged to attend weekly lab meetings to learn more about these areas of interest.
Requirements:
At least a 3.5 GPA
Available for 9 hours per week
How to apply:
Please use the following link to fill in an application and upload your resume/CV: https://fsu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_00rdaa2aPlZDvbE
