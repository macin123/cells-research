Latest News
February 9, 2026, Our Perspective, “Emerging Zero-Dimensional Organic Metal Halide Hybrids for X-ray Scintillation and Direct Detection,” is now published in ACS Energy Letters as part of the special issue “The Evolving Landscape of Energy Research: Insights from Leading Researchers.” 
February 6, 2026, FSU News highlights our research on a report “FSU researchers develop new materials for next-generation X-ray technologies”. 
February 3, 2026, The team “Roentgen Matters” led by Dr. Ma has been selected for participation in National Science Foundation I-Corps™ 2026 Winter Cohort 2 and an NSF I-Corps™ Teams Award. The project is “Organic Metal Halide Hybrids for Radiation Scintillation and Detection”. 
December 15, 2025, Our work “Amorphous Zero-Dimensional Organic Metal Halide Hybrid Scintillators with High Light Yield and Fast Response” has been published in Angewandte Chemie International Edition. 
December 15, 2025, Our collaborative work with Dr. Bin Ouyang’s group “A Predictive Framework for Discovering Organic Metal Halide Hybrids beyond Perovskites” has been published in Chemistry of Materials. 
December 09, 2025, Our work “Semiconducting Organic Metal Halide Complex Glasses for Efficient X-Ray Detection” has been published in Small. 
November 07, 2025, Oluwadara Olasupo successfully defended his dissertation “Organic Metal Halide Hybrids and Complexes for Direct X-ray Detection”. Congratulations, Dr. Olasupo!
