Dr. Michael Cortez is currently recruiting new graduate students for Fall 2023.
Research and Professional Interests:
I use mathematical models to explore how adaptation and species interactions drive patterns observed across communities. Much of my past work has focused on predator-prey, host-pathogen, and other exploiter-victim systems. I develop theory using a combination of analytical methods and numerical simulations, with much of my work being done in collaboration with empiricists. Please see my lab page for more information.
