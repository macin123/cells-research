Materials Physics Laboratory Est. 2013 
Research that sheds light on the matter
Working at the interface of condensed-matter physics, materials science, and modern experimental methods, we study light–matter interactions in unconventional semiconductors to help build quantum technologies of tomorrow.
A collaborative, interdisciplinary, and training-focused lab culture that prepares students for research careers in industry, national labs, and academia.
Join us 
Research Endeavors
Our current research, using AI-driven autonomous experiments, seeks to understand nonreciprocal and nonlinear responses in low-dimensional, hybrid and symmetry-breaking semiconductors.
Symmetry Breaking in Hybrid Crystals
Exploring emergent phenomena in noncentrosymmetric hybrid crystals.
Low-Dimensional Materials for Nonlinear Optics
Discovering new low-dimensional materials with enhanced nonlinear optical responses.
Next-Generation Optoelectronics
Developing advanced optoelectronic devices based on hybrid semiconductors.
Autonomous Lab for Scientific Research
Designing and implementing AI-driven autonomous laboratories for high-throughput experimentation.
