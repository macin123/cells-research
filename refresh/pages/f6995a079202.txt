Project Description
Are you interested in building AI chatbots for children’s STEM learning program at public libraries through two research projects, funded the Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS)? The first project, entitled Family Makers, focuses on integrating an AI chatbot within public library engineering programs for collaborative use by parents and children without librarian facilitation. The second project aims to develop a GenAI-integrated Mathematizing Public Library Storytimes (MPLS) program that can increase children’s (3-5 years) algebraic reasoning, as well as caregivers’ and public librarians’ self-efficacy in math literacy skills. Once developed, AI chatbots will be tested at children’s STEM programming at two public libraries.
Research Tasks: AI chatbot development for two projects; data collection at public libraries; data analysis
Skills that research assistant(s) may need: Required qualification:
• Previous experience developing an AI chatbot (or similar applications)
• Valid driver’s license and access to reliable personal transportation.
Recommended qualification:
• Previous experience working with children
Mentoring Philosophy
Mentoring is a big part of my professional identity, and I am very excited to collaborate with students at all levels. I take great fulfillment in guiding students as they transition into critical thinkers and independent researchers. To support this, I employ a research-apprenticeship model rooted in learning-by-doing approach. Students will work side-by-side with me through data collection and analysis and have ample opportunity to observe real-world research practices while actively engaging in them. I have witnessed that this research-apprenticeship model provides students a safe and supportive environment to experiment, learn, and grow as a researcher and a critical thinker.
