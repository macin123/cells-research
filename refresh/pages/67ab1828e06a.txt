Undergraduate Research Opportunities
Rat Behavior Lab 
DIS opportunity with rat behavior:
Faculty Mentor(s):
Instructors for EXP3422
Prerequisites: completed Conditioning and Learning (EXP 3422), permission of instructor, online training modules (completed prior to access to registration code for course).
Students will gain hands-on experience handling and training multiple rats in conditioning procedures and will be able to conduct data analysis under the guidance of a research mentor.
Students should contact their course instructor for EXP3422 for a recommendation and more information about this DIS.
Program
Neuroscience
Research Area
Conditioning and Learning with rats. This DIS offers additional training with the conditioning and learning lab rats used in EXP3422. Students must have already completed EXP3422.
