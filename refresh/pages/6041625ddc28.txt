Project Description
This research project examines how school principals in Nepal make sense of school–community partnerships. In Nepal’s public-school system, principals carry multiple leadership and administrative responsibilities. One of their most significant roles is serving as a bridge between the school and its community. Principals who serve as the secretary of the School Management Committee (SMC), a formal governance body mandated by national policy for all public schools. In this capacity, principals play a central role in facilitating communication, collaboration, decision-making, and relationships between schools and community members.
Prior research has explored school–community partnerships and various dimensions of principal leadership, limited attention has been given to how principals themselves interpret, understand, and enact their roles in these partnerships. This study tries to address that gap by examining principals’ sensemaking processes related to school–community partnerships in Nepal. The study will draw on Institutional Review Board (IRB)-approved data. 
Research Tasks: We will do a literature review and data analysis.
