Research Opportunities 
Postdoctoral Researchers 
If you are interested in a postdoctoral position, please send your CV and 2 letters of recomendation requested on your behalf to Prof. Hu ( yhu@fsu.edu ). Candidates with training background in energy materials including rechargeable batteies, flow batteries, all-solid-state batteries, and fuel cells, and knowledge in solid-state NMR spectroscopy are encouraged to apply.
Graduate Students 
Undergraduate students interested in pursuing a Ph.D. in chemistry should first apply directly to the Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry Graduate Program . After receiving admission to the Graduate program, please email Professor Hu if interested in arriving early to participate in summer research (prior to the start of the fall semester). Current FSU graduate students that are interested in joining the Hu Research Group should email Professor Hu to set up a meeting or stop by her office in CSL 3004.
Undergraduate Students 
Undergraduate students interested in Directed Individual Study (DIS) or Honors Research (4 hours in the laboratory per week = 1 credit) should email Professor Hu a brief statement of goals and interests, an unofficial transcript, and an updated resume or CV. Prior to joining the group and beginning research, undergraduate students are required to have passed general chemistry (CHM 1045 or equivalent), taken FSU safety training and been enrolled at FSU for at least one semester. Students are expected to make a multi-semester commitment to their research projects with the goal of publishing original research results.
