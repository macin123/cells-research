Harmon Youth (Harmony) Lab 
Are you passionate about understanding and improving mental health outcomes for children? 
The Harmon Youth (Harmony) Lab is seeking motivated research assistants to join our team. 
The Harmony Lab seeks to gain a deeper understanding of how children experience and manage their emotions. Using a developmental psychopathology approach, we explore the various factors that contribute to emotion dysregulation. Our research uses a range of methods, including multi-informant rating scales, clinical interviews, computer-based tasks, and psychophysiological measures, to assess cognitive, behavioral, and emotional functioning in children. Our work is transdiagnostic, covering a range of childhood disorders such as anxiety, depression, ADHD, and other emotional and behavioral problems. As part of our ongoing studies, the Harmony Lab also offer clinical services to children and families. By integrating clinical practice with research, we aim to discover more effective ways to improve treatment for youth. Our current project focuses on identifying mechanisms associated with the development and maintenance of irritability and emotional disorders in children. Our lab is part of FSU’s Center for Translational Behavioral Sciences. 
Responsibilities: 
Administer standardized questionnaires 
Obtain informed consent 
Assist with neurocognitive data collection and processing 
Manage and organize research data 
Conduct literature reviews 
Engage in community outreach and participant recruitment 
Additional Opportunities Include: 
Gaining hands-on experience with diverse methodologies 
Working with children and families 
Engaging in interdisciplinary research 
Working closely with faculty and graduate students 
Contributing to scientific publications and presentations 
This is a great opportunity to work with families in our community and gain valuable research and clinical experience. 
Requirements: 
Minimum GPA of 3.2 
Commitment of 9 hours per week (3 credit hours a semester) 
Availability and commitment for at least 3 consecutive semesters (e.g., Spring 2026 - Fall 2026) 
Interested candidates should apply should use this link to submit an application: https://fsu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_aarMpM9Wgvr75EG 
Please reach out to harmonylab@psy.fsu.edu if you have any questions.
