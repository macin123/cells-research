Project Description
Examining the potential financial hardships transfer student experience when transferring to FSU. This data will be used to inform University leaders, donors, and staff. 
Research Tasks: Interviews, data collecting, coding, research question development
Skills that research assistant(s) may need: Ability to build rapport req
College level writing skills req 
Transfer student rec 
Mentoring Philosophy
It is important to me that students have equal ownership of the project. Meaning, it is not just my study but it is OUR study. I want students to feel invested in the work they do, and also recognize that the data they collect, interpret, and disseminate in this study impact their peers for classes to come.
