The Riddle Lab is hiring a PhD student for Fall 2027 and undergraduate research assistants (for Spring 2027).
Mentorship statement : Justin is committed to training the next generation of scientists. Justin and you will set goals for your success and work together to reach specific milestones. In addition to learning and growing as a scientist, you will learn leadership skills and develop towards independence. As a member of the lab, you will uphold the values of the lab: commitment to scientific integrity, support for your fellow lab members, and assist in fostering an inclusive intellectual environment.
Please send inquiries to Justin Riddle, jriddle@fsu.edu, include a statement of your interest and your CV.
We are always looking for talented undergraduates . We have a rolling application process.
