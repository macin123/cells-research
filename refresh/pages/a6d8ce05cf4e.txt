Get Involved
Join our team and contribute to groundbreaking relationship science research
The Contextual and Automatic Influences on Relationships (CAIR) Lab is always seeking dedicated research assistants to join our team. We offer opportunities for students to participate for DIS credit or on a volunteer basis. Our lab is committed to fostering a supportive environment where you can gain valuable research experience while advancing your career goals.
We understand the demands of student life, which is why we offer flexible hours to accommodate your schedule. Whether you&#x27;re looking to build research skills, explore the field of relationship science, or prepare for graduate school, the CAIR lab is here to support you.
Join us and be part of cutting-edge research that makes a real difference in understanding and improving relationships!
Why Join Our Lab?
Research That Matters: Our work directly impacts how we understand and improve relationship outcomes, contributing to more fulfilling relationships, physical and mental health, and overall well-being.
Hands-on Experience: As a research assistant, you can gain valuable research experience using innovative methodological approaches rooted in theories of social cognition, health psychology, and relationship science.
Career Development: Build essential skills in research methodology, data analysis, and scientific communication while working with experienced mentors.
Flexible Hours: We offer adaptable schedules to fit your academic and personal commitments.
Supportive Environment: Our lab is dedicated to helping you succeed in your academic and professional goals.
