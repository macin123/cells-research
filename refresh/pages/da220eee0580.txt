Gary K. Ostrander Ph.D.
Professor
gary.ostrander@med.fsu.edu 
Main Campus 
Biosketch
Dr. Ostrander has held faculty positions at Oklahoma State University, Johns Hopkins University, the University of Hawaii, and Florida State University, actively contributing to teaching, research, and service. His administrative roles include Associate Dean of the Graduate School at OSU, Associate Provost for Research and Chair of the Graduate Board at Johns Hopkins, and Vice Chancellor for Research and Graduate Education at the University of Hawaii. He also served as Interim Dean of the University of Hawaii's Medical School (2004–2006) and President of its Practice Plan. From 2012 to 2020, he was Vice President for Research and President of the Florida State University Research Foundation.
Dr. Ostrander's research spans cancer biology to coral reef biology. He earned his doctorate from the University of Washington (UW) and was an NIH postdoctoral fellow in UW's Department of Pathology. His early research leveraged aquatic species to explore novel aspects of the biology of cancer, leading to current studies on DNA modifications as predictors of breast cancer risk. As the Principal Investigator, he has secured ~$25M in NIH funding, published 85+ peer-reviewed articles, edited four books, co-authored a field guide, and holds 9 patents.
He has also written and served as the PI on two Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) grants (~$4M) aimed at fostering undergraduate researchers, with a strong emphasis on increaseing diversity among scientists. These initiatives partnered with minority-serving institutions, including the University of Texas-El Paso, University of Alaska-Fairbanks, University of Guam, Morgan State University, and Coppin State University.
