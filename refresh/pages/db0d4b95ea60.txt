Associate Professor

Main Campus 
Jia Lab 
Biosketch
Dr. Jia's research has two related focuses: 1) using structural biology to elucidate how the HIV-1 virus evades host immunity; 2) developing therapeutics to inhibit viral immune evasion and thus empower the immune cells to clear HIV-1 infection. Dr. Jia first established his independent research group in 2016 at University of Massachusetts Dartmouth. He and his group moved to Florida State University College of Medicine in Spring 2025.
Education
B.S. in Chemistry, Peking University
M.S. & Ph.D. in Chemistry, Michigan State University
Postdoctoral training, Yale University
Publications
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/myncbi/1BAXViDbGpM5r/bibliography/public/
